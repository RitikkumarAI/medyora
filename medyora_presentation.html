Viva Technical Defence
System Edge: Concurrency, No-Show & Competitive Moat
16 / 18
CHALLENGE 01
"Why Not Just Call the Doctor?"

• Phone Calls are Blind: 80% clinic phone lines are busy; receptionists maintain inaccurate paper registers with zero live delay tracking.

• Practo's Static Slot Lie: Practo gives a static "10:15 AM" slot, but consultations fluctuate (8-25 mins). Patients still wait 2 hours in crowded lobbies.

• Medyora's Live Telemetry: Like Uber tracks cabs, Medyora tracks your live token (#11 in cabin, #14 you, 18 min ETA) so you only walk in 5 mins before your turn.

CHALLENGE 02
"If 3 Patients Book the Same Slot?"

• Atomic 5-Min Mutex Lock: When Patient 1 clicks a slot, a distributed lock (Redis/PostgreSQL SELECT FOR UPDATE) temporarily freezes it across all clients.

• Engine Unique Constraint: Composite DB constraint (doctor_id, date, slot_time) makes double-booking mathematically impossible at the database engine level.

• Smart Instant Waitlist: Patients 2 & 3 are instantly offered the next micro-batch token or placed on auto-notifying priority waitlist.

CHALLENGE 03
"Patient Books but Never Shows Up?"

• Doctor 1-Click "Skip No-Show": If absent past 5-min grace, doctor skips patient; next token is immediately called so zero clinical time is wasted.

• 1-Tap "Running Late" Swap: Patient stuck in traffic taps "Running Late" to swap to an afternoon slot without penalty, freeing their current slot.

• Economic & Score Deterrents: ₹100 prepaid discount drives commitment; unexcused no-shows reduce Patient Reliability Score (PRS).
